# Example Profile: Beach Cars And Runabouts

Use this as the default niche profile or replace it with your own.

Budget:

- About $30,000 total excluding shipping.
- A $1,000-$2,000 stretch is acceptable for an exceptional fit.
- Use current bid/ask plus buyer fee for screening.

Primary lanes:

- Family local runabout: beach trips, school runs, short errands, ideally usable with kids.
- Solo town runabout: smaller, charming, easier around town than a full-size motorcycle or car.
- Curiosity: oddballs worth watching even if they are not a perfect practical fit.

Prioritize:

- VW Thing, Mini Moke, Citroen Mehari, Fiat Jolly-style cars.
- Suzuki Samurai, Sidekick, Geo/Chevy Tracker, Isuzu Amigo, Daihatsu Rocky.
- Jeep CJ/YJ/TJ/LJ/JK, older 4Runner, Mitsubishi Montero, Delica, kei trucks/vans.
- Subaru BRAT/Baja, air-cooled VW convertibles, golf carts/NEVs/electric resort cars.
- Vespas/scooters, Honda Monkey/Grom/Trail/Cub-style bikes, old Harleys, BMW airheads, sidecars, quirky EV conversions.

Deprioritize:

- Clearly over-budget icons, high-end restomods, heavy off-road builds, huge lifts, salvage/title issues, major rust, and non-running projects.

Diligence:

- Title/VIN/history, rust/underbody, accident records, driving videos, road manners, EV battery/range/charging, seat belts/restraints, passenger legality, insurance, and registration constraints.
