# Valuation Guide

Every included listing should have:

- `fair`: a fair hammer/current-sale value range plus buyer-fee-inclusive estimate.
- `ceiling`: a practical stop price as hammer and buyer-fee-inclusive total.
- `conf`: `low`, `medium`, or `high`.
- `currentTake`: cheap, fair, rich, stretch, bargain watch, or similar.
- `dossier.valueScore` (optional): a `0..1` position on the CHEAP..RICH meter — `0` = clearly cheap for what it is, `0.5` = mid-band, `1` = priced rich. Derive it from the same comp window you used for `fair`. Omit it if the market is too thin to place.

Use:

- recent same-platform comparable sales when available
- broader model-market context
- condition, mileage, originality, modifications, location, title status, and rust/body risk
- active bid trajectory and time remaining

Do not pretend precision. Prefer ranges like `$18k-$22k fair hammer / about $18.9k-$23.1k with fee`.

For thin markets, say confidence is low and explain the uncertainty in `concerns` or the report.
