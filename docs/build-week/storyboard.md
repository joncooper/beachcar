# Beachcar Build Week video storyboard

Working title: **It found the one**
Category: **Apps for Your Life**
Target runtime: **2:49**
Export ceiling: **2:55** (the official limit is under three minutes)

## Editorial promise

In less than three minutes, a judge should understand one complete idea: Beachcar turns a user's local Codex into a private auction scout, GPT-5.6 supplies the judgment, Beachcar supplies durable taste and a focused iPhone decision surface, and the loop already surfaced a car its creator bought.

The emotional proof and the technical proof should be intercut, not presented as separate stories. Open with the real 1960 Willys DJ-3A Surrey outcome, prove one run from Codex to iPhone, then show how feedback changes the next run.

## Visual rules

- Frame: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Keep all essential type inside a 10% title-safe margin.
- Palette: warm pink `#ef5b91`, paper `#f7f2e8`, ink `#080a0b`, and restrained mint. Mint is a small confirmation accent or pale wash, never a fluorescent field.
- Car first. No moon, starscape, chrome fantasy, or decorative scenery competing with the Surrey.
- Use hard cuts, short lateral moves, and two or three purposeful punch-ins. Avoid template transitions.
- Captions are always on. Burn in a reviewed caption track for the public upload and attach an accurate YouTube transcript.
- Default audio is the original voiceover with light original interface sounds. Do not add music unless its license is documented.
- Never show passwords, tokens, email addresses, notifications, browser profiles, account menus, or private listing comments.

## Two-cut asset policy

The **public rights-safe cut** is the shipping default. It uses original generated Surrey art, fresh Beachcar app captures, scrubbed Codex/MCP captures, and creator-shot footage or photos. It contains no auction-page capture, marketplace logo, or listing-gallery image.

The **permission-ready BaT alternate** keeps the same timing and narration. If written publication permission is received from the relevant copyright holder, it may substitute at most five selections from the user-reported 87-photo listing gallery. Do not copy, download, edit, or place any additional gallery image into the timeline while permission is pending. See [photo-permissions.md](photo-permissions.md).

## Continuity capture checklist

The 1:10–1:34 section is the credibility hinge. Record it as one session, even if the final cut uses punch-ins.

1. Start local Codex with GPT-5.6 and the Beachcar skill visible.
2. Show `get_setup_context`, then the signed-in browser-control step without exposing third-party copyrighted page content.
3. Show `submit_candidates` returning five accepted candidates.
4. Show `publish_run` returning the final run ID.
5. Show `verify_latest_run` returning that same ID and candidate count.
6. Open the iPhone run and show the same sanitized ID and five cards.
7. Capture a second take with cursor movement slowed for editing safety.

Use the real returned identifier in the footage. Do not type a decorative replacement into the UI. It may be partially masked consistently for privacy, but the visible suffix must match across all three views.

## Capture list

- Generated Surrey hero, transparent background and full-card variants.
- Creator-shot real-car photo or five-second video, if available.
- iPhone: taste onboarding, desktop handoff, shortlist, Surrey dossier, Love, Never again.
- Desktop: Codex setup, exact MCP calls, passing verification, next context after feedback.
- `/demo` scenes: proof, research, review, learn at 1920×1080.
- Build Week making-of: primary Codex task, concise diff, test summary, browser replay, iOS Simulator.
- End card with final URL and no placeholder text.

## Final editorial gates

- Runtime is between 2:40 and 2:55 after the final audio tail.
- The narration explicitly says what was built, how Codex works inside the product, how GPT-5.6 is used, and how Codex helped build the extension.
- The run ID and count match across publish, verify, and iPhone.
- `DETERMINISTIC REPLAY` remains visible anywhere fixture footage appears.
- Every third-party image, logo, music cue, and font has a documented license, or it is removed.
- The upload is understandable with sound only, captions only, or both.
- The final frame and video description say **Apps for Your Life**.
